Commonly Asked

Koi bhi confusion? Yahan clear karte hain sab kuch.

Kya donation ke baad NEETopia mera data use karega?
Bilkul nahi. UPI transactions directly bank-to-bank hote hain — hum aapka naam, account number kuch bhi store nahi karte. Transaction completely private hai. Hum na spam karte hain, na data sell karte hain.
Kya donation tax deductible hai?
Abhi NEETopia registered NGO nahi hai, isliye donation tax deductible nahi hai 80G ke under. Lekin hum isko future mein proper structure dene ki koshish kar rahe hain. Aapka support is journey ka hissa hai.
Agar main donate karta/karti hoon toh kya platform pe koi special access milega?
Nahi — aur ye intentional hai. NEETopia ka principle hai ki platform completely equal hona chahiye. Donor hoga toh extra feature nahi milega, aur non-donor ko koi disadvantage nahi. Donation purely platform ko alive rakhne ke liye hai, access ke liye nahi.
Kya donation refund ho sakti hai?
Agar galti se payment hua hai ya koi technical issue hai, toh hello@neetopia.in pe contact karo with your UPI transaction ID. Hum within 48 hours respond karenge. Genuine cases mein refund process karte hain.
Kya main monthly donation set kar sakta/sakti hoon?
Abhi automatic recurring donation ka system nahi hai — lekin aap manually har mahine donate kar sakte ho. Agar aap ₹99-₹249/month dena chahte hain toh calendar reminder set kar lo — aur hum grateful rahenge! Future mein subscription-style support laane ka plan hai.
Agar main donate nahi kar sakta/sakti toh bhi NEETopia free use kar sakta hoon?
Haan — 100%. NEETopia always free rahega, aur koi guilt nahi honi chahiye. Agar aap donate nahi kar sakte, toh platform share karo dosto ke saath. Word-of-mouth bhi donation jaisi help hai. Bas padho aur NEET crack karo — yahi hamara goal hai.
