How to Give This Test — Sahi Tarika
1
Mode Select Karo — Full (100 Qs), Quick (50 Qs), ya PYQ Style (30 Qs) choose karo apni zaroorat ke hisaab se.
2
Timer ascending chal raha hai — koi time limit nahi. Apni pace mein solve karo. Timer sirf track karta hai kitna time liya.
3
Question Navigator use karo — upper right button se kisi bhi question pe jump karo. Answered questions green highlight honge.
4
Submit karo jab ready ho — sab questions attempt karne ke baad "Submit Test" dabaao. Detailed results aur explanations milenge.
5
Review aur Download karo — results ke baad PDF download karo. Wrong answers ko retry karo taaki weak areas strong ho jayein.
6
NEET Scoring — Real NEET pattern follow karta hai. Har sahi jawab ke liye +4, galat ke liye −1. Skip karne par 0.
