FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

NEET MCQs effectively kaise practice karein?
NCERT-based chapter MCQs se shuru karo. Har galat answer analyze karo — NCERT wapas kholo. Error notebook maintain karo. PYQs tab solve karo jab chapter-wise practice 80%+ complete ho. Speed se pehle accuracy — negative marking mein guessing costly hai.
NEET ke liye daily kitne MCQs solve karney chahiye?
Beginners: 50–70 MCQs/day. Intermediate: 100–120 MCQs/day timed sessions ke saath. Advanced (final 3 months): 150+ MCQs/day + hafte mein 2 full mocks. Quantity se zyaada analysis ki quality matter karti hai.
Kya PYQs NEET preparation ke liye enough hain?
PYQs essential hain — NEET ka 60–70% past years se repeat hota hai. Lekin akele PYQs enough nahi hain. Inhe chapter-wise topic MCQs aur assertion-reason questions ke saath combine karo for complete syllabus coverage.
NEET mein konse subject ko sabse zyaada practice chahiye?
Biology (360 marks — 90 questions) ko sabse zyaada practice volume chahiye. Physics mein consistent numerical drilling chahiye — score differentiator hai. Chemistry mein NCERT line-by-line reading + reactions aur periodic trends pe MCQs karo.
NEET accuracy kaise improve karein?
Guessing band karo — unanswered question 0 deta hai, galat −1. Concept clarity strengthen karo — har error pe NCERT wapas kholo. Weekly mistake log revise karo. Timed practice se exam pressure simulate karo aur decision speed build karo.
