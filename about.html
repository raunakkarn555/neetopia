Our Story

Kyun banaya NEETopia?

NEET prepare karna India mein already bahut mushkil hai — lekin isse aur mushkil bana dete hain woh platforms jo har cheez ke paise maangte hain. Notes ke paise. PYQs ke paise. Ek chapter padhhne ke liye subscription.

NEETopia isi frustration se paida hua. Mission simple hai: jo student NEET crack karna chahta hai — usse kisi cheez ke liye paise nahi dene chahiye. NCERT-based notes, PYQs, video lectures, study plans — sab kuch ek jagah, bilkul free.

Hum koi big startup nahi hain. Koi VC funding nahi hai. Yeh platform pure passion se banaya gaya hai — medical aspirants ke liye, medical aspirants ki taraf se. Agar yeh platform helpful laga, toh server costs ke liye ek chai ki kimat donate kar sakte ho — warna bilkul zaroorat nahi.

What We Stand For

Hamare principles
