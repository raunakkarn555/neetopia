Marking Scheme

Marks kaise milte hain?

NEET mein negative marking hoti hai. Ek galat jawab seedha ek sahi jawab cancel karta hai — isliye educated guess sahi, random guess galat strategy hai.

+4
Correct Answer
Har sahi jawab par 4 marks milte hain. Accuracy hi sab se bada weapon hai NEET mein.
−1
Wrong Answer
Galat jawab par 1 mark katta hai. Ek galat = ek sahi waste. 4 galat = ek sahi cancel.
0
Unattempted
Agar answer nahi pata toh chhodna better hai. No marks gained, no marks lost.

Negative marking ka dhyan rakho: 4 galat jawab ek sahi jawab cancel kar dete hain. Agar question mein 60–70% confidence nahi hai toh chhodna better hai — random guessing score girata hai.

Time Strategy

200 minutes kaise divide karo?

Sahi time allocation mein Biology pehle aata hai — wahan se sabse zyada marks hain aur confidence bhi milta hai baaki paper ke liye.

Phase 01
0 – 20 min
Biology — Scan
Poora Biology paper ek baar quickly scan karo. Easy questions mark karo, tough wale skip karo for now. First impression mein hi 30+ questions identify ho jaate hain.
⏱ 20 minutes · Scan only
Phase 02
20 – 80 min
Biology — Complete
Marked easy questions pehle attempt karo. Phir medium difficulty. Very tough ones pe 2 min se zyada mat lago — move on karo. Sab 45 questions attempt karne ki strategy banao, skipping carefully.
⏱ 60 minutes · Full Biology
Phase 03
80 – 140 min
Chemistry
Inorganic aur Organic pehle — ye zyada conceptual hain. Physical Chemistry ke numerical baad mein. Har question pe 80 seconds average rakhne ki koshish karo.
⏱ 60 minutes · Full Chemistry
Phase 04
140 – 200 min
Physics + Revision
Physics attempt karo — ye sabse time-consuming hota hai toh iska buffer last mein sahi rahta hai. Last 15 min mein no new questions — sirf revision karo aur marked questions re-check karo.
⏱ 60 min · Physics + 15 min revision

Common Mistakes

Yeh galtiyan mat karna

Har saal lakhs students in basic mistakes ki wajah se marks loose karte hain. Ye sirf awareness se avoid ho jaate hain.

5 Critical Mistakes

In sab ko exam se pehle internalize kar lo

01 Negative marking ignore karna — Ek galat answer = −1 mark. 4 galat answers = ek sahi answer ka poora fayda zero. Agar 2 options tak eliminate kar sako toh attempt karo, warna chhod do. Random guessing kabhi mat karo — expected value strictly negative hai.
02 Physics mein pehle jaana aur wahan ghanta lagana — Biology se 360 marks aate hain, Physics se sirf 180. Physics mein 30 min waste karna = double loss. Hamesha Biology pehle attempt karo — max marks wahan hain, aur questions typically faster hote hain.
03 Unattempted questions mein randomly guess karna — 4 options mein random guess karne ka expected value = (1×4 + 3×−1) ÷ 4 = +0.25. Yeh bhi tabhi jab truly random ho. Agar thoda bhi clueless ho toh chhodna numerically better hai — marks protect karo.
04 Last 10 minutes mein naye questions attempt karna — Last 10–15 min sirf revision ke liye hain. Naya question attempt karna = rushed answer = higher chance of negative marking. Jo already marked hai usse carefully check karo — wahan zyada marks milenge.
05 OMR sheet mein galat bubble bharna — NEET mein OMR correction allowed nahi hai. Rough cutting ya overwriting = cancelled. Har question ke baad OMR carefully fill karo — last mein bulk mein mat bharo. Ek careless OMR mistake = 4 marks direct loss.
