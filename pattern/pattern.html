Marking Scheme

Marks kaise milte hain?

NEET mein negative marking hoti hai. Ek galat jawab seedha ek sahi jawab cancel karta hai — isliye educated guess sahi, random guess galat strategy hai.

+4
Correct Answer
Har sahi jawab par 4 marks milte hain. Accuracy hi sab se bada weapon hai NEET mein.
−1
Wrong Answer
Galat jawab par 1 mark katta hai. Ek galat = ek sahi waste. 4 galat = ek sahi cancel.
0
Unattempted
Agar answer nahi pata toh chhodna better hai. No marks gained, no marks lost.

Section B important rule: Agar Section B mein 10 se zyada questions attempt karo toh only the first 10 attempted count honge — remaining automatically cancelled. Isliye carefully 10 best questions choose karo aur wahi attempt karo.

Time Strategy

200 minutes kaise divide karo?

Sahi time allocation mein Biology pehle aata hai — wahan se sabse zyada marks hain aur confidence bhi milta hai baaki paper ke liye.

Phase 01
0 – 20 min
Biology — Scan
Poora Biology paper ek baar quickly scan karo. Easy questions mark karo, tough wale skip karo for now. First impression mein hi 30+ questions identify ho jaate hain.
⏱ 20 minutes · Scan only
Phase 02
20 – 80 min
Biology — Complete
Marked easy questions pehle attempt karo. Phir medium difficulty. Very tough ones pe 2 min se zyada mat lago — move on karo. Section B mein best 10 carefully choose karo.
⏱ 60 minutes · Full Biology
Phase 03
80 – 140 min
Chemistry
Inorganic aur Organic pehle — ye zyada conceptual hain. Physical Chemistry ke numerical baad mein. Har question pe 80 seconds average rakhne ki koshish karo.
⏱ 60 minutes · Full Chemistry
Phase 04
140 – 200 min
Physics + Revision
Physics attempt karo — ye sabse time-consuming hota hai toh iska buffer last mein sahi rahta hai. Last 15 min mein no new questions — sirf revision karo aur marked questions re-check karo.
⏱ 60 min · Physics + 15 min revision

Common Mistakes

Yeh galtiyan mat karna

Har saal lakhs students in basic mistakes ki wajah se marks loose karte hain. Ye sirf awareness se avoid ho jaate hain.

5 Critical Mistakes

In sab ko exam se pehle internalize kar lo

01 Section B mein 10 se zyada attempt karna — Agar 12 questions mark karte ho, sirf pehle 10 count honge. 2 questions waste, aur agar 11th ya 12th galat tha toh bhi wo count nahi hoga — par confusion zaroor hoga.
02 Negative marking ignore karna — Ek galat answer = ek sahi answer cancel. 4 galat = ek extra sahi questionka fayda zero. Doubt hai toh chhodna better hai — unless elimination se 2 options tak aa sako.
03 Physics mein time zyada lagana aur Biology neglect karna — Biology se 360 marks aate hain, Physics se 180. Physics mein stuck rehne se double loss hota hai — time bhi, marks bhi. Biology pehle karo.
04 Unattempted questions mein randomly guess karna — Random guess mein chance of being correct is 25%, chance of losing 1 mark is 75%. Expected value negative hai. Don't guess randomly.
05 Last 10 minutes mein naye questions attempt karna — Last 10-15 min sirf revision ke liye hain. Naaya question attempt karna = rushed answer = higher chance of negative marking. Jo already marked hai usse check karo.
