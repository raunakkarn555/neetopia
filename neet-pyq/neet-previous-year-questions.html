Strategy

How Toppers Use PYQs

PYQs are not just practice — they're the blueprint. Here's the systematic approach that actually gets you 650+.

01
Identify Repeating Questions

Many NEET questions repeat with 2–3 word changes across years. Solving 5–6 years of PYQs reveals these patterns clearly. These questions are guaranteed to appear again.

02
Time Yourself Like a Real Exam

180 questions in 200 minutes = ~67 seconds per question. Practice each year paper in one sitting with a timer. This trains your speed and decision-making under pressure.

03
Master Elimination Technique

NEET has negative marking (-1 per wrong). In MCQs, you can often eliminate 2 options immediately. Practicing PYQs sharpens this skill — it can gain you 15–20 extra marks.

04
Subject-wise Revision Cycles

After each chapter revision, solve that chapter's PYQs from all years. This shows exactly what NTA focuses on — saving you 40% revision time vs reading the full chapter again.

05
Wrong Question Bank

Bookmark every question you get wrong. Revisit your wrong-question bank 3 days before the exam. These are your highest-probability improvement areas.

06
Mock-Test Style Practice

Once a week, simulate exam conditions: no phone, no breaks, 200 min timer. This is the single most powerful practice method to improve your actual NEET score.
