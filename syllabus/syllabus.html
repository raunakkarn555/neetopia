📊 Exam Structure

180 Questions · 720 Marks · 200 min

Biology 90 Qs (360) · Chemistry 45 Qs (180) · Physics 45 Qs (180). Sab questions compulsory — koi Section A/B nahi.

🎯 Marking Scheme

+4 Correct · −1 Wrong · 0 Unattempted

Negative marking applies to all subjects equally. Unattempted questions mein koi penalty nahi. Agar 2 se zyada options nahi eliminate kar sakte toh chhodna better hai.

📚 Syllabus Basis

NCERT Class 11 & 12

Syllabus NCERT textbooks Class 11 & 12 pe based hai — Physics, Chemistry, Biology. NCERT ke bahar se direct questions lagbhag nahi aate. NCERT first, reference books second.
