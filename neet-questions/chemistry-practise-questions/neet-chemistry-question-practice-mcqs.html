Har tarah ka question — ek jagah

NEET Chemistry mein sirf straight MCQs nahi aate. Physical Chemistry mein numericals, Organic mein mechanisms, Inorganic mein fact-based — sab covered hain.

NCERT Line Questions
Inorganic Chemistry ka 95% NCERT se aata hai. Exact definitions, properties, reactions from NCERT converted to MCQ format. Especially for p-Block, d-Block, and Coordination Compounds.
High Yield
Reaction Mechanism MCQs
Organic Chemistry mechanism-based questions — SN1, SN2, electrophilic addition, EAS, nucleophilic addition. Step-by-step reasoning required. Most scoring format in Organic.
Organic Focus
Physical Chemistry Numericals
Numericals for Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics — mole concept, molarity, pH, rate law, EMF, colligative properties. Calculator-free practice essential.
Physical Chem
Assertion–Reason
Two-statement questions testing conceptual depth — especially for Chemical Bonding, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, and Coordination Compounds.
NEET Format
PYQs 2013–2024
12 years of actual NEET Chemistry questions — chapter-wise sorted. PYQ practice is the single most effective prep strategy. Patterns repeat, especially in Organic and Inorganic.
Most Important

Tricky Format
Rapid Revision MCQs
40-question rapid-fire sets per chapter — designed for last-2-month revision. Covers all key facts, formulas, and reactions quickly.

Full 25-question chapter-level tests with timer. Simulates real exam pressure. Accuracy tracked, weak areas highlighted. Inorganic aur Organic ke liye especially useful.

Strategy

Chemistry toppers kaise practice karte hain

Chemistry mein sirf MCQs solve karna kaafi nahi — Physical, Organic aur Inorganic ke liye alag approach chahiye. Yeh strategies 145+ Chemistry scorers use karte hain.

01
3-Part Split Strategy
Chemistry 3 alag subjects hai. Physical mein formulas aur numericals roz practice karo. Organic mein mechanisms samjho, reactions yaad karo. Inorganic mein NCERT line-by-line padhna mandatory hai — sirf facts hain.
Time allocation: Organic 40% · Physical 35% · Inorganic 25%. Organic gives maximum marks per hour of practice in NEET.
02
Organic — Mechanism First
Reactions yaad karne ki zarurat nahi — mechanisms samjho. Agar mechanism pata hai (electrophilic addition, nucleophilic substitution, EAS), toh product predict kar sakte ho. GOC aur Hydrocarbons pehle master karo.
Named reactions list: Aldol condensation, Cannizzaro, Haloform, Clemmensen, Wolf-Kishner, Reimer-Tiemann, Friedel-Crafts, Balz-Schiemann — yeh sab NEET PYQs mein repeat karte hain.
03
Physical — Formula Sheet Banao
Ek dedicated formula sheet banao for Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium. Har chapter ke saare formulas ek page pe. Daily ek baar review karo. Numericals mein formula recall hi sabse common mistake hai.
NEET trick: Physical Chemistry numericals mein mostly 1–2 step calculations aate hain — never 5-step. Koi bhi answer jo bahut lambi calculation le, woh galat approach hai.
04
Inorganic — NCERT Line-by-Line
p-Block, d-Block, Coordination Compounds — inhe NCERT se bahar jaana nahi. Sirf NCERT. Har paragraph mein bold/italic text alag se padho. Exceptions aur anomalous behaviour — yahi NEET mein aata hai.
High-value topics: Oxyacids of halogens & sulphur, interhalogen compounds, CFSE values, EAN rule, geometrical isomers in square planar complexes — ye sab NEET repeat karte hain.
05
PYQ Chapter-wise Solving
Year-wise mat karo — chapter-wise karo. Jab chapter padh lo toh usi chapter ke 12 saal ke PYQs solve karo. Chemistry mein patterns bahut repeat hote hain — especially Coordination Compounds aur Organic reactions.
Key insight: Aldehyde/Ketone reactions, p-Block properties, aur Coordination isomers se milke 8–10 NEET questions har saal aate hain — inhe master karo.
06
Weak Chapter Deep Dive
Jis chapter mein accuracy 50% se kam ho, woh chapter completely restart karo NCERT se. Chemistry mein ek concept miss ho toh poora chain break hota hai — especially Equilibrium aur Chemical Bonding mein.
Common weak spots: Hybridisation of p-Block compounds (PCl₅, SF₆, XeF₄), Nernst equation numericals, Half-life calculations, Complex ion nomenclature.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Chemistry practice ke baare mein jo questions sabse zyada pooche jaate hain — sab yahan hain.

Chemistry 3 parts mein divide karo — Physical, Organic, Inorganic. Physical mein formulas roz revise karo aur numericals daily practice karo. Organic mein mechanisms samjho, reactions yaad karo. Inorganic mein NCERT line-by-line padhna mandatory hai. PYQs chapter-wise solve karo. Roz 40 MCQs target karo — 15+15+10 split.
High weightage: Chemical Bonding (3-4 Qs), Thermodynamics (3-4 Qs), Equilibrium (3-4 Qs), p-Block Groups 15-18 (3-4 Qs), Coordination Compounds (3-4 Qs), Electrochemistry (2-3 Qs), Chemical Kinetics (2-3 Qs), Organic GOC+Hydrocarbons (4-5 Qs), Aldehydes/Ketones (3-4 Qs). Collectively Organic gives 15-18 questions out of 45.
Inorganic ke liye NCERT 95% sufficient hai — pure facts hain. Organic ke liye NCERT concepts + reaction practice zaroori hai. Physical ke liye NCERT + additional numerical practice (NCERT examples nahi kafi). Kisi bhi extra book se pehle NCERT completely master karo.
NMC ne yeh chapters NEET 2026 syllabus se officially hata diye: Solid State (Class 12), States of Matter (Class 11), Surface Chemistry (Class 12), Hydrogen (Class 11), s-Block Elements (Class 11), General Principles of Isolation of Elements / Metallurgy (Class 12), Polymers (Class 12), Chemistry in Everyday Life (Class 12), Environmental Chemistry (Class 11). Inhe bilkul mat padho.
Pehle GOC (Chapter 9, Class 11) completely master karo — electronic effects, hybridisation, isomerism sab. Phir Hydrocarbons, phir Haloalkanes, phir Alcohols/Phenols, phir Aldehydes/Ketones/Amines. Har chapter ke named reactions ki dedicated list banao. Roz 5-6 named reactions revise karo. NCERT examples solve karo — most Organic PYQs directly NCERT examples se aate hain.
