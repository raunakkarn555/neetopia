Practice Formats

Har tarah ka question — ek jagah

NEET Biology mein sirf straight MCQs nahi aate. Yahan sab question formats covered hain — exactly jaise exam mein aate hain.

NCERT Line Questions
Directly from NCERT text — exact lines, definitions, and statements converted to MCQ format. Most NEET questions trace back here.
High Yield
Assertion–Reason
Two-statement questions testing conceptual depth. Practice A–R for all high-weightage chapters — Genetics, Ecology, Cell Biology.
NEET Format
Diagram Questions
Label-based and interpretation MCQs from key Biology diagrams — cell, heart, nephron, flower, DNA. Yeh questions 8–10 marks worth hain.
Visual MCQs
Match the Following
Column-matching format common in Classification, Hormones, Enzymes, Ecology topics. Often tricky — practice carefully.
Tricky Format

Strategy

Biology toppers kaise practice karte hain

Sirf MCQs solve karna kaafi nahi — ek systematic approach chahiye. Yeh strategies 650+ scorers use karte hain.

01
NCERT Line-by-Line Reading
NEET Biology ka 90% NCERT se aata hai. Pehle poora chapter line-by-line padho. Har definition, har table, har figure caption important hai. Underline karo, marker lagao.
Mnemonic trick: Taxonomic hierarchy — Keep Pots Clean Or Family Gets Sick (Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species)
02
Diagram Mastery Strategy
Blank paper pe baar baar diagrams banao — nephron, heart, sarcomere, flower, DNA double helix. Label karo bina book dekhe. Yeh directly 8–10 NEET marks ke liye hai.
Pro tip: Har chapter ke diagrams ek dedicated notebook mein banao. Exam se pehle sirf woh notebook revise karo.
03
PYQ Chapter-wise Solving
Year-wise mat karo — chapter-wise karo. Jab chapter padh lo toh usi chapter ke 12 saal ke PYQs solve karo. Patterns dikhenge, repeated topics pehchaan aayenge.
Key insight: Genetics + Molecular Biology ke PYQs mein 40% questions repeat hote hain — exact same concept, different wording.
04
Spaced Repetition Revision
Chapter padha → next day revise → 3 din baad revise → 7 din baad revise → 14 din baad revise. Har revision ke saath MCQs bhi solve karo. Isse yaaddasht pakki hoti hai.
Tool tip: NEETopia progress tracker se apna revision schedule track karo — chapter-wise completion dikh jaata hai.
05
Assertion–Reason Approach
A–R questions mein pehle dono statements alag alag evaluate karo. Pehle decide karo kaunsa T/F hai, phir decide karo relationship sahi hai ya nahi. Hurry mat karo.
Common trap: Both statements true but Reason explains Assertion galat ho sakta hai. Option (b) vs (a) — relationship check karna mat bhoolo.
06
Weak Chapter Identification
Jis chapter mein accuracy 50% se kam ho, woh chapter dobara NCERT se start karo. MCQs galat hone par sirf correct answer nahi — full concept samajhna zaroori hai.
Tracking: Har galat MCQ ka topic note karo — week ke end mein pattern dekhoge. Wohi tumhara weakest topic hai.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Biology practice ke baare mein jo questions sabse zyada pooche jaate hain — sab yahan hain.

Pehle NCERT line-by-line padho, phir chapter-wise MCQs solve karo. Assertion-reason questions roz practice karo. Diagrams har 3 din mein revise karo. PYQs topic-wise solve karo, year-wise nahi. Roz 50 MCQs ka target rakho aur accuracy track karo. Consistent practice hi sabse best strategy hai.
Haan — approximately 90% NEET Biology questions directly NCERT se aate hain. Har line, har table, har diagram caption important hai. However, sirf NCERT padhna enough nahi — assertion-reason aur match-the-following formats bhi practice karne zaroori hain jo NCERT concepts ko different angles se test karte hain.
Beginners: 30–40 MCQs/day. Intermediate: 50–70 MCQs/day. Advanced/Revision phase: 90–120 MCQs/day. Pehle accuracy focus karo (70% se upar), phir speed badhao. Weak topics track karo aur unhe har week dobara practice karo.
Spaced repetition use karo: 1 din, 3 din, 7 din, 14 din ke baad revise karo. NCERT diagrams pe focus karo — sab diagrams label karo. Har chapter ka ek-page summary banao. PYQs chapter-wise solve karo high-weightage topics identify karne ke liye. Mnemonics use karo classification aur taxonomy ke liye.
High weightage chapters: Genetics & Evolution (14–16 questions), Human Physiology (12–14), Plant Physiology (8–10), Reproduction (8–10), Ecology (8–10), Cell Biology (6–8). Ye chapters milke 60%+ Biology marks cover karte hain. Inhe pehle master karo.
