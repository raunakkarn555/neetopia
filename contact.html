Quick Answers

Common Questions

Jo sawaal aksar pooche jaate hain — unke answers yahan hain.

Kya NEETopia pe contact karna zaruri hai doubt clear karne ke liye?
NEET subject doubts ke liye seedha chapter pages pe jaao — wahan NCERT-based explanations hain. Contact sirf platform ke baare mein hai — content errors, suggestions, bugs. Subject doubts ke liye hum individual tutoring nahi dete.
Kya koi chapter request kar sakte hain jo abhi available nahi hai?
Bilkul! Email karo aur subject line mein "Chapter Request" likho. Hum requests note karte hain aur priority ke hisaab se add karte hain. Jitne zyaada log ek chapter request karenge — utna pehle add hoga.
Maine ek content error dhundha — kaise report karein?
Email karo — subject line mein "Content Error" likho aur body mein exact chapter, topic aur correction clearly likho. NCERT reference bhi do agar possible ho. Hum 48 hours mein verify karke fix karte hain.
Kya NEETopia volunteer ya contributor ban sakte hain?
Haan! Agar tum content likhna chahte ho, notes banana chahte ho, ya koi aur tarike se help karna chahte ho — seedha email karo. Subject line mein "Contribute" likho. Hum hamesha passionate contributors ka swagat karte hain.
Mera data safe hai — form fill karne ke baad kahan jaata hai?
Form ka data sirf contact ke liye use hota hai — sell, share, ya store nahi kiya jaata. Email optional hai — reply chahiye toh do, nahi chahiye toh chhod do. NEETopia ki privacy policy mein full details hain.
